# DocFerry 0.0.66: the full DocFerry experience

This is the major Community update from the former free-service client to the
complete Bondie-powered DocFerry product. It updates the existing `docferry`
plugin in place; it is not a second plugin or a compatibility build.

## Highlights

- Sign in with a Bondie account through your system browser and return directly
  to DocFerry.
- Save DocFerry Shares, public web pages, videos, and audio from one simple
  **Save to Obsidian** entry point.
- Publish and manage secure Markdown Shares with stable links, passwords,
  expiration, updates, stopping, and inactive-history deletion.
- Pro adds Folder Share, Obsidian-inspired reader styling, higher Share limits,
  and Advanced Import for enabled article and media sources.
- Advanced Import runs in the background, can be cancelled or resumed after a
  restart, shows a preview before writing, and allows 30 accepted jobs per
  calendar month with one job processing at a time.
- Note, generated media-note, and folder readers now share one responsive
  presentation system with corrected mobile spacing and no internal renderer
  labels.
- The Dashboard provides membership, plans, Shares, support, and a secondary
  path to Bondie account and privacy controls.

## Free and Pro

The plugin remains useful on Free: save public links, import DocFerry Shares,
and publish up to five active single-note Shares. Pro provides 20 active note
Shares, five active Folder Shares, larger files, theme styling, and Advanced
Import. Monthly and yearly Pro plans have the same product benefits.

Obsidian Community should classify DocFerry as **Optional payments**.

## Existing users

This is a one-way move to `https://docferry.bondie.io`.

- Sign in with a Bondie account after updating; the former product session is
  intentionally not reused.
- Your vault notes are not removed or rewritten.
- Existing links remain governed by the service that created them until that
  service is retired.
- Historical `df_*` metadata from another service is not sent to Bondie. To
  manage a note in the new product, publish it again and receive a new Share.

See [Moving from the free-service release](../docs/MIGRATION-FROM-FREE.md) and
[Privacy](../PRIVACY.md) before updating a managed installation.
